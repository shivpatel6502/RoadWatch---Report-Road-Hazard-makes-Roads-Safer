Our Mission

Today, citizens report road hazards through informal channels — Google Maps pins, social media posts, phone calls — that offer no status tracking, no accountability, and no way for the community to signal "this affects many of us." Road crews have no visibility into which problems citizens consider most urgent.

RoadWatch changes that — by giving citizens a structured voice, city admins a prioritised work queue, and the community real-time visibility into road repairs.

Platform Features

Everything you need to report, track, and resolve road hazards.

Photo Upload

Up to 3 photos per report, avatar upload, supporting documents — all stored securely.

AI Severity Scoring

Every report gets an AI severity score (1–10) and duplicate detection to avoid redundant submissions.

Search & Filter

Keyword search across title, description, city, street. Filter by type, severity, status, and city.

Community Upvotes

One upvote per user per report — collective voting surfaces the most impactful hazards first.

Real-time Notifications

In-app alerts when a followed report changes status. Never miss an update on your hazards.

Analytics Dashboard

Charts, KPIs, resolution rate, severity trends, and hazard type breakdowns — all in one view.
